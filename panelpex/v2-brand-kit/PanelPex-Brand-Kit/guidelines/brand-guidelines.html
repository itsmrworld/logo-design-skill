01 · The mark

Two panels, one licence.

The symbol is a ligature of the two P's in PanelPex, welded into one block. Each P carries a square panel window; inside each window sits the other letter's colour — licences passed from one panel to the next.

  • Heavy, flat letterforms read at favicon size.
  • Sharp chamfer + soft radii: precise, but approachable.
  • Ink and violet halves stay readable in one colour thanks to a hairline split.

02 · Logo versions

Lockups & symbol

Primary: horizontal colour lockup. Use stacked in square formats, the symbol where the name is shown nearby, one-colour versions for print, stamps and embroidery.

03 · Clear space & minimum size

Give it room

Clear space: keep at least the width of one P stem (≈ 17 % of the symbol width) free on every side; more is always better.

Minimum size:
Symbol — 16 px (use symbol-small-notiles below 32 px) · 8 mm print.
Horizontal lockup — 120 px wide · 25 mm print.
Stacked lockup — 80 px wide · 18 mm print.

Usage

Ink + white for surfaces, violet for actions and highlights, light violet on dark backgrounds. Keep violet under ~20 % of any layout.

06 · Please don't

Misuse

Don't stretch or squash.

Don't rotate or tilt.

Don't place the colour version on dark — use the reversed one.

Don't use clashing background colours.

Also avoid: recolouring the halves, adding shadows or outlines, rebuilding the wordmark in another font, or cropping the symbol.
