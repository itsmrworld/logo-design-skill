# Reference ads analysed (competitor, Bangladesh hosting company) — patterns, not assets to copy

## A. "10 GB SSD hosting + free .COM domain" (square)
- Photoreal composite: three black server racks standing on a rock in a cloudy sky, jungle foreground → dramatic "sky / outdoor" scene.
- Blue rounded highlight box (white text) for line 1, plain dark headline for line 2; round badge with the price on the left; footer bar: contact label, phone with icon, "Visit Now + URL" with globe icon.
- Takeaway: strong real-world hero + highlight box + footer bar.

## B. Seasonal offer with person on a beach (square)
- Lifestyle photo (palm trees, sea, man with laptop) + a server tower; headline with blue highlight box and dark stroke; huge price block in a blue panel ("মাত্র ২০৳ Monthly" style); phone bottom-left, URL bottom-right.
- Takeaway: emotion/lifestyle + giant offer typography + simple footer. (We do not use prices unless provided.)

## C. "BDIX Premium Hosting GOLD" (square)
- Dark blue studio, enormous white word "GOLD" fading behind a glossy 3/4-view server stack with floor reflection and light shaft; pill-badge headline; 2×4 grid of outlined spec pills (CPU, cores, RAM, NVMe, BDIX, bandwidth, terminal, backup); blue bottom bar with "Visit now" + phone.
- Takeaway: giant word behind hero, spec-pill grid, dark glow lighting, solid bottom bar. This became `post-hero-server-rack.py`.

## D. Facebook cover (SiteChai-style)
- Deep navy gradient with thin diagonal lines; logo+name top-left; large two-line headline; right side a tilted dashboard, phone, floating widgets (domain bar, WordPress, SSL, Database, Page-speed gauge) fading at the bottom.
- Our version: `cover-3d-dashboard.py` (own widgets, own palette, content kept inside the mobile/desktop safe zone).

## Psychology used
Ownership words ("Your"), single focal point, contrast between headline and background, green "available/ready" confirmation, clear CTA pill with arrow, concrete spec pills (rational proof), real-map data viz (global credibility).
